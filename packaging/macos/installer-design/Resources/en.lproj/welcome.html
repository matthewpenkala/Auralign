This installer will place Auralign in your Applications folder and its exact verified offline bootstrap resources in the system Application Support folder.

After installation, Auralign guides you through one native Complete Offline Setup pass for your selected speech recognition pack. No Terminal or token is required. Processing is local and offline after that exact pack verifies.

Close any running copy of Auralign before continuing.
